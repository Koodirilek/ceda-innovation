Privacy notice

This notice explains how the CEDA Innovation Portal handles personal data under Botswana’s Data Protection Act, 2024.

What we collect and why

  • Your name, email address and phone number, to create and secure your account and to send you alerts.
  • The ideas, files and replies you submit, so they can be evaluated and decided.
  • Activity records (who did what, when and from where), to keep the portal secure and accountable.

How we protect it

  • Role-based access: people only see what their role allows. Evaluators review ideas without seeing who submitted them.
  • Passwords are stored as salted hashes. Sign-in codes expire after 10 minutes.
  • Data is held in Botswana and is not sent to other countries.

How long we keep it

Only as long as needed for the innovation programme and legal obligations. Ask CEDA to delete your account and data at any time.

Your rights

You may ask to see, correct or delete your personal data, and to withdraw consent for alerts in your account settings.
